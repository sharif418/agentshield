# Code of Conduct

AgentShield aims to be a serious, useful, and respectful open-source project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas, tradeoffs, and evidence.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction quickly.
- Keep security discussions responsible and private when appropriate.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or trolling.
- Public disclosure of sensitive vulnerabilities before maintainers have had a reasonable chance to respond.
- Spam or low-effort promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and community.
